The place next door

Make yourself at home

Nabo means neighbour. That's the whole idea.

Order a few plates for the table and pass them around. Norwegian and French cheeses, slow-cooked brisket, the catch of the day, tacos on nixtamal tortillas. Pick a drink you like and stay as long as you want.

Sit at the tiled bar and watch the open kitchen, or sink into a velvet booth in the back room under the palms.
